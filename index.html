hierforecast

Hierarchically reconciled energy demand forecasts using MinT shrinkage reconciliation, scored on pinball loss. Regional forecasts sum exactly to the national figure and the upper tail stays accurate.

Independent forecasts disagreed by up to 612 MW; after reconciliation the gap is float round-off.
