About

Where Data, Analytics and AI Meet

I turn open-ended business questions into the KPIs, models, pipelines and dashboards that teams use to decide.

At Natera I own six analytics workstreams, among them Voice of Patient, AI Disposition, Salesforce transfer reporting and Twilio/Calabrio reconciliation. I work in SQL and Python on Snowflake, Databricks and BigQuery, build the reporting in Power BI, Tableau and Sigma, and take the findings to Operations, Product, Finance, Sales and Support.

At Con Edison I lead the modernization of a mission-critical gas-electric incident notification system. That means running workshops with GIS, control-center and engineering leaders, writing the BRD/FRD, and building agreement on the 200-ft/24-hour proximity logic before UAT and rollout.

At PSEG I built Fleet Forecaster with React, Flask, PostgreSQL, Python and Excel/VBA to model fleet demand, capacity, cost and budget scenarios. It cut a 6-hour planning run to 5 minutes and saves about $120K a year.

Earlier, in India, I spent three years in client analytics. At ASH‑TECH Solutions I built financial and performance models and automated the analytics with SQL, Python, dbt and Airflow across Salesforce, HubSpot and NetSuite data, work that generated $200K+ in client value. At WebScape I turned ambiguous client requests into scoped requirements and dashboards, and standardized processes that cut onboarding time by 30%.

I also publish research on AI systems and design patterns with co-authors at Pace University and NYU, including GRAFT, an agent framework for edge and personal devices (ACM CAIS 2026), and Branch-Commit-Validate, a Git-inspired workflow for red-team agents (Springer, SAM 2025).
